# Brief — trip-planner

**From:** the founder
**Received:** 2026-08-18T09:18:30Z

## The idea, verbatim

a travel agent AI webapp that plans your itinerary for a given timeframe. On the homepage it asks what kind of vacation you want (mountain, beach, party, honeymoon, peace etc), then dates, then budget, then number of people, then dynamically asks further questions backed by a graph/tree (international vs national, then narrowing further) to zoom in on the user's perfect trip. Based on the answers it fetches flight, hotel and experience data from aggregators (Google Flights, Skyscanner, other open aggregators) to assemble the trip. Must be somewhat deterministic and scalable in future. Goal is to find the perfect vacation around the budget but without a strict cutoff.
